Privacy policy

Stream Scheduler, a desktop program by Leaven Labs. Last updated November 15, 2026.

In short

Stream Scheduler runs on your computer and has no server. Leaven Labs does not receive, collect, store, sell or share any of your data. There are no accounts with us, no analytics and no advertising.

What the app accesses, and why

YouTube. When you connect a channel, you sign in on Google's own page and allow the app to manage your YouTube account. The app uses this only to read your channel's name and its stream keys' names, and to create the scheduled live broadcasts of your schedules (their title, description, start time and visibility). It does not read your videos, comments, viewers or other account data.

Facebook. When you connect a Page, the app uses an access token for that Page only to read the Page's name and to create the scheduled live videos of your schedules.

Wix. When you connect a Wix website, the app uses an API key you create to read one collection of that site and to write the stream's link and title into one item of it, so that your page shows the coming stream.

Where data is kept

Everything stays on your computer. Your schedules, the names and ids of your channel and Page, and the history of created streams are in a settings file in your user folder. Sign-in tokens and client secrets are in your operating system's keychain (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, or the Linux Secret Service). Stream keys are not saved.

Who data is sent to

The app sends requests only to Google (YouTube), Wix and Meta (Facebook), each only if you connected it, over HTTPS, to do what is described above. Nothing is sent to Leaven Labs or to anyone else.

Removing access and deleting data

Remove a destination in the app to delete its sign-in from your computer. You can also revoke the app's access at any time in your Google account's security settings, for Wix by deleting the API key in your Wix account, and for Facebook under Settings → Business integrations. Deleting the app's settings folder deletes everything else.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it with YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
