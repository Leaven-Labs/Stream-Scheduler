Stream Scheduler

Privacy policy

Stream Scheduler, a desktop program by Leaven Labs. Last updated November 15, 2026 (version 0.0.5).

In short

Stream Scheduler runs on your computer and has no server. Leaven Labs does not receive, collect, store, sell or share any of your data. There are no accounts with us, no analytics and no advertising.

What the app accesses, and why

YouTube. When you connect a channel, you sign in on Google's own page and allow the app to manage your YouTube account. The app uses this only to read your channel's name and its stream keys' names, and to create the scheduled live broadcasts of your schedules (their title, description, start time and visibility). It does not read your videos, comments, viewers or other account data.

Facebook. When you connect a Page, the app uses an access token for that Page only to read the Page's name and to create the scheduled live videos of your schedules.

Wix. When you connect a Wix website, the app uses an API key you create to read one collection of that site and to write the stream's link and title into one item of it, so that your page shows the coming stream.

WordPress and other websites. When you connect a WordPress website, the app uses an application password you create to read the site's list of synced patterns and to write the coming stream into one of them. When you connect a web address (a webhook), the app sends each new stream's title, description, start time and YouTube link to that address.

Alerts. If you set up a way to be told (email, Discord, Slack or Pushover), the app sends messages there about your streams: their title, time and link, and the reason when something failed. They go from your computer straight to the service you chose.

New versions. Once a day the app asks GitHub for this project's public list of releases. The request carries only the app's name and version. You can switch this off in Settings.

Where data is kept

Everything stays on your computer. Your schedules, the names and ids of your channel and Page, and the history of created streams are in a settings file in your user folder. Sign-in tokens, client secrets, application passwords, webhook addresses and the mail password are in your operating system's keychain (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, or the Linux Secret Service). Stream keys are not saved.

Who data is sent to

The app sends requests only to Google (YouTube), Wix, Meta (Facebook), your WordPress website, a web address you entered, and the alert services you set up, each only if you connected it, and to GitHub for the list of releases unless you switched that off. All of it is encrypted in transit and does only what is described above. Nothing is sent to Leaven Labs or to anyone else.

Removing access and deleting data

Remove a destination in the app to delete its sign-in from your computer. You can also revoke the app's access at any time in your Google account's security settings, for Wix by deleting the API key in your Wix account, and for Facebook under Settings → Business integrations. Deleting the app's settings folder deletes everything else.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it with YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
